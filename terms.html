Why we use it

We use this information to review submissions, contact contributors, operate and secure the catalogue, handle corrections and rights complaints, keep an accountability record, and improve the service. We do not sell contributor personal data.

Approved catalogue entries may publicly display the resource title, description, course, semester, topics, type, attribution supplied for publication, and external resource link. We do not intend to publish contributor email addresses or notes marked for reviewers. Do not put private information in a public-facing title, description, file, or link.

Google Forms and Google Drive process data under Google’s own terms and privacy policy, potentially on systems outside Oman. Only provide information and files you are permitted to process in this way.
