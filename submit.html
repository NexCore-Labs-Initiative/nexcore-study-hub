Community contributions

Useful material deserves a clearer home.

This first beta year is focused on gathering useful SQU study resources. Share notes, slides, study guides, practice material, or carefully organized course resources; every submission is manually reviewed before it can appear in the catalogue.

The review process

A simple route from contribution to catalogue.

  1. Submit the details

    Tell us the course, semester, topic, resource type, and provide the Google Drive link.

  2. We review the entry

    NexCore checks that the information is useful, clear, and appropriate to share.

  3. We publish the catalogue record

    Approved material receives a verified catalogue entry. The file stays in its original Google Drive location.

Ready to contribute?

Send a resource for review.

Review and accept the contribution terms before continuing.

Only submit material you created or have permission to share. Do not submit copyrighted textbooks, active or leaked assessments, instructor-only material, answer keys shared without permission, or files containing personal or confidential information.

Resource reports

Need a resource corrected or removed?

If a listing is inaccurate, unavailable, or should not be shared, contact NexCore with the resource title or URL and a short explanation. We will review the request.
