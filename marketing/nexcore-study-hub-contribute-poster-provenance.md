# NexCore Study Hub contribution poster

- Purpose: Social poster encouraging SQU classmates to contribute study resources.
- Canvas: 1080 × 1350 pixels (4:5 portrait).
- Public contribution URL: `https://nexcore-study-hub.vercel.app/submit.html`
- QR generation: `qrcode@1.5.4`, error correction level H, four-module quiet zone.
- Brand source: Current Study Hub badge, palette, typography hierarchy, cards, badges, and rounded surfaces from the website.
- Exact-content method: Poster copy, URL, logo geometry, and QR are deterministic SVG elements; no generative image model was used.
- Safeguard: The footer identifies the site as independent and reminds contributors to share only permitted material.
